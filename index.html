THE SOUNDBOARD FOR PEOPLE YOU HATE

Fire-alarm chirps, chalkboard physics, and a paid pack of kids yelling “skibidi.” All sounds are synthesized — no stolen clips.

Why these sounds? Surveys and psychoacoustics keep landing on the same villains: 3.1 kHz smoke-alarm beeps, acoustically “rough” alarms (40–80 Hz modulation), knife/fork-on-glass, chalkboard scrape (2–5 kHz), chewing, yapping, snoring, and neighbor-grade domestic noise. The slang pack is the other modern consensus: skibidi, 6-7, rizz, Ohio, sigma, Fanum tax, gyatt, delulu, unc. Checkout is simulated for this prototype — nothing is billed.
